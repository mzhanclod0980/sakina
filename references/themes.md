# Themes — starting points for the ayah, story and hadith

These are **starting points, not scripts.** Choose what fits the root you found in the interview, not the surface topic. A person «anxious about exams» may really be carrying «I'm worthless if I fail» — then the self-worth ayahs fit better than the anxiety ones.

Always fetch the ayah text (see `quran-source.md`) and the hadith wording (see `hadith-source.md`; keys in backticks). Hadith numbers are sunnah.com numbering; a hadith not in the bundle — paraphrase it without a number unless you verified it.

## Anxiety, worry, fear of the future
- 13:28 — hearts find rest in the remembrance of Allah.
- 65:2–3 — whoever is mindful of Allah, He makes a way out for him and provides from where he does not expect; whoever relies on Allah, He is enough for him.
- 94:5–6 — with hardship comes ease (twice).
- 2:286 — Allah does not burden a soul beyond its capacity.
- Story: Musa at the sea, trapped between the sea and Pharaoh's army — «Never! My Lord is with me; He will guide me» (26:61–62). The Prophet ﷺ in the cave with Abu Bakr: «Do not grieve, Allah is with us» (9:40).
- Hadith: the Prophet's ﷺ dua seeking refuge from worry and grief, helplessness and laziness, cowardice and miserliness, debt and being overpowered by people (al-Bukhari 6369).
- Rizq / work / studies: 11:6 (no creature on earth but its provision is on Allah); 51:22; hadith of the birds that leave hungry in the morning and return full (at-Tirmidhi 2344).

## Sadness, grief, loss
- 2:155–157 — trials of fear, hunger, loss; «Indeed we belong to Allah and to Him we return»; blessings and mercy upon the patient.
- 12:86 — Yaqub: «I only complain of my grief and sorrow to Allah.» Grief brought to Allah is not weakness.
- 93 (ad-Duha) — revealed when revelation paused and the Prophet ﷺ felt left alone: «Your Lord has not forsaken you, nor is He displeased» (93:3); «what comes after is better for you than what came before» (93:4). Strong for «I feel abandoned by Allah».
- Story: the Prophet ﷺ wept for his son Ibrahim (al-Bukhari 1303); the Year of Grief, when he lost Khadijah and Abu Talib.

## Grief with guilt towards someone who died («I didn't make it in time», «I should have…»)
- Their good deeds can continue: «When a person dies, his deeds end except for three: ongoing charity, beneficial knowledge, or a righteous child who prays for him» (Muslim 1631). Dua for them reaches them.
- A man asked whether to give charity on behalf of his mother who died suddenly — the Prophet ﷺ said yes (al-Bukhari 1388).
- Good step: a letter to the deceased with what was left unsaid, and a small charity or dua in their name. It turns guilt into action.
- 12:86 (Yaqub) fits when grief comes with anger or «where was God?» — the question itself can be brought to Allah.

## Guilt, sin, shame, «Allah won't forgive me»
- 39:53 — «O My servants who have transgressed against themselves, do not despair of Allah's mercy; Allah forgives all sins.»
- 25:70 — for those who repent and do good, Allah replaces their bad deeds with good.
- 4:110 — whoever does wrong then seeks forgiveness will find Allah Forgiving, Merciful.
- Story: Adam and Hawwa — «Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves…» (7:23). Musa after killing a man by accident: «My Lord, I have wronged myself, so forgive me» — and He forgave him (28:16).
- Hadith: «Every son of Adam errs, and the best of those who err are those who repent» (at-Tirmidhi 2499). Hadith qudsi: «O son of Adam, as long as you call upon Me and hope in Me, I will forgive you whatever you have done, and I don't mind» (at-Tirmidhi 3540).
- Careful: guilt that is healthy leads to repentance and change; shame that says «I am bad» paralyses. Work with which one it is.

## «I'm a hypocrite», all-or-nothing, falling short in worship
- 64:16 — «Be mindful of Allah as much as you are able» — Allah asks for *your* «as much as you can», not perfection.
- Companion story: Hanzala came out saying «Hanzala has become a hypocrite!» because at home with his family he didn't feel what he felt with the Prophet ﷺ. The Prophet ﷺ answered: «O Hanzala, an hour for this and an hour for that» (Muslim 2750). A changing state is not hypocrisy.
- The Prophet ﷺ and his companions once slept through Fajr on a journey and woke to the heat of the sun; no one was called a hypocrite — they moved on from that place and prayed (Muslim 680). «Whoever sleeps through a prayer or forgets it, let him pray it when he remembers.»
- A hypocrite doesn't suffer over a missed prayer — the suffering itself is a sign of iman. Then look for the practical cause (sleep, schedule) and name it plainly.

## Low self-worth, comparison, «I'm not enough»
- 95:4 — We created man in the best of stature.
- 17:70 — We have honoured the children of Adam.
- 49:13 — the most honoured of you with Allah is the most mindful of Him — not the richest, most successful or most beautiful.
- Hadith: «Allah does not look at your appearance or your wealth, but at your hearts and your deeds» (Muslim 2564).
- Envy: 4:32 — do not wish for what Allah has given some of you over others.

## Loneliness, feeling unseen
- 2:186 — «When My servants ask you about Me — I am near; I answer the call of the caller when he calls Me.»
- 50:16 — We are closer to him than his jugular vein.
- Story: Yunus in the darkness of the whale: «There is no god but You, glory be to You, I was among the wrongdoers» — and We answered him and saved him from distress, «and thus We save the believers» (21:87–88).

## Resentment, hurt by someone, difficulty forgiving
- 12:92 — Yusuf to the brothers who threw him in a well: «No blame upon you today.»
- 24:22 — «Let them pardon and overlook. Do you not love that Allah should forgive you?»
- 41:34 — repel evil with what is better, and the enemy may become a close friend.
- 42:40 — the recompense of a wrong is its equal, but whoever pardons and makes peace, his reward is with Allah. (Forgiveness is a choice, not an obligation to accept ongoing harm.)
- Forgiving ≠ denying the pain ≠ staying in harm. It frees the heart.

## Anger
- 3:134 — those who restrain anger and pardon people; Allah loves the doers of good.
- Hadith: «The strong one is not the one who overcomes people by wrestling, but the one who controls himself when angry» (al-Bukhari 6114). «Do not get angry» — repeated three times (al-Bukhari 6116).

## Parents and family
- 17:23–24 — not even «uff» to them; «My Lord, have mercy on them as they raised me when I was small.»
- 31:14–15 — accompany them in this world with kindness even when they disagree with you in faith.
- 29:8 — kindness to parents, but no obedience in disobeying Allah.
- New Muslims with non-Muslim family: 31:15 was revealed in connection with Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, whose mother pressured him when he accepted Islam (per Al-Mukhtasar) — the command was to stay close with kindness, not to cut off and not to hide one's faith. Companion story: Asma bint Abi Bakr's mother, still a polytheist, came to visit; the Prophet ﷺ told her: «Yes, keep ties with your mother» (al-Bukhari 2620).
- For talking about religion with family → `family-dawah.md`.

## Marriage and relationships
- 30:21 — spouses created for tranquillity, with affection and mercy between them.
- 2:187 — they are a garment for you and you are a garment for them.
- 4:19 — live with them in kindness; if you dislike something in them, Allah may place much good in it.
- Aisha said the Prophet ﷺ never struck anyone with his hand — not a woman, not a servant (Muslim 2328).
- Abuse is never «a test to be patient with» → `safety.md`.

## Doubts about faith
- 2:260 — Ibrahim asked Allah to show him how He gives life to the dead, «so that my heart may be at rest.» Asking is not shameful — a prophet asked.
- Hadith: companions feared thoughts too terrible to speak; the Prophet ﷺ said «That is clear faith» (Muslim 132) — being disturbed by such thoughts is itself a sign of faith.
- 3:190–191 and 41:53 — invitation to reflect on creation and on oneself.
- Never argue or shame. Listen to the actual doubt; often it is pain («why did Allah let this happen»), not philosophy.

## Emptiness, loss of meaning, «why do I live»
- 51:56 — I did not create jinn and humans except to worship Me (worship in its broad sense: a life lived towards Allah).
- 67:2 — He created death and life to test which of you is best in deeds.
- 23:115 — did you think We created you without purpose?
- If emptiness comes with hopelessness or thoughts of not wanting to live → `safety.md` first.

## Bad habits, addiction, relapse
- 39:53 — do not despair of Allah's mercy.
- 29:69 — those who strive for Us, We will surely guide them to Our ways.
- 3:135 — those who, when they commit an indecency, remember Allah and seek forgiveness, and do not persist knowingly.
- Hadith: «The most beloved deeds to Allah are the most consistent, even if small» (al-Bukhari 6464; Muslim 783). Small, steady steps beat big promises.
- Real addiction needs real help (doctor, support group) — say so.

## Rumination, «if only I had…»
- Hadith of the strong believer: strive for what benefits you, seek Allah's help, don't give up; and if something befalls you, don't say «if only I had done…», but say «Allah decreed and did what He willed», for «if only» opens the door to Shaytan (Muslim 2664). Then return to what can be done now.

## Hardship as good, «why me»
- Hadith: «How wonderful is the affair of the believer — all of it is good: if good befalls him he is grateful, and that is good for him; if harm befalls him he is patient, and that is good for him» (Muslim 2999).
- 2:216 — you may dislike something while it is good for you.
- Illness: Ibrahim — «when I fall ill, He heals me» (26:80); Ayyub — «harm has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful» (21:83–84).

## Laziness, procrastination, low motivation
- 94:7–8 — when you are free, strive; and to your Lord direct your longing.
- The dua against helplessness and laziness (al-Bukhari 6369).
- Check first: is it laziness, or exhaustion, depression, fear of failure? Treat the root.
